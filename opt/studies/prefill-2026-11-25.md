# Chunked Prefill Research & Design Study — Inkling-2-Mini on GTX 1060 (sm_61)

Date: 2026-11-25  
Branch: `t3/prefill-chunked-study` (base `9c735bd`)  
Hardware: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 6 GB (`sm_61`, 10 SMs, 192 GB/s DRAM, ~3.8 TFLOP/s FP32, PCIe 3.0 x16 ~11.5 GB/s pinned H2D)  
Model: Inkling-2-Mini (24 layers, 48 layer-visits/token: 6 prelude + 3×12 loop + 6 tail; 40 SWA-1024 + 8 full-attn visits; `n_embd=2304`, `nh=24`, `hkv=8`, `hd=128`, 128 MoE experts, top-8, `expert_ff=1152`, EXL3 trellis weights, 10 loop layers `L6..15` on-device = 2,796 MiB, 14 layers host-pinned = 3,475 MiB).

---

## Executive Summary

1. **Current prefill is slower than decode**: one-at-a-time `step()` encodes at **32.6–40.8 ms/tok (24.5–30.6 tok/s)** across 512–8K tokens. Skipping per-token 201K-logit D2H (`step_id`) saves **3.30 ms/tok** (29.3 ms/tok at 512).
2. **Existing `step_block` (`NT=8`) is 1.6–1.9× SLOWER than serial `step()`** (**52.6–78.1 ms/tok**, **12.8–19.0 tok/s**) and **exceeds the 5,080 MiB VRAM cap** (5,406 MiB peak at ctx=512). It also required 3 bugfixes just to run cleanly (`kernels.cu:5741` swapped hit/miss `skipmode` across `ev_join`; `runner.rs:1747` `clip_l2` and `runner.rs:1951` `ctrl_apply` assumed `nt==1`). Its bottleneck is `inkwell_exl3_gemv_moe_ntok` (**40.8–49.9 ms/tok**, 62–65% of GPU time), because `INKWELL_MOE_NKY=2` disables `exl3v2` and falls back to an un-tiled dp4a loop, while `serial` uses SASS-tuned `ch2nopless` (`12.4–13.5 ms/tok`).
3. **`step_block` is NOT bitwise-identical to `step()`**: every logit differs (`kl_sb = 3.53e-5` at 512 tok, `4.25e-4` at 64 tok), KV differs in 68–69% of F16 elements (`K_max_abs = 10.83` at 512 tok due to rare MoE top-8 router boundary flips), and 64-token greedy generation diverges at token 56/64 (`md5` does **not** match).
4. **Loop-MoE state is NOT recurrent across tokens**: `prelude`, `bank`, `bank_out`, `msgs`, `blocks`, and `control()` are strictly per-token across the 3 loop passes (`runner.rs:2444-2481`). The only cross-token ops are (a) causal 1D depthwise FIR convs (`sconv_l_cache=4`, trivially parallel over a chunk) and (b) causal attention over KV.
5. **Recommended design (`C = 256–512` tiled chunked prefill)**: grouped-by-expert register-tiled EXL3 GEMM + batched dense EXL3 GEMM + GQA-3 FlashAttention + parallel FIR conv + double-buffered PCIe H2D streaming. Grounded in `sm_61` microbenchmarks (`prefill_kbench`), `C = 512` predicts **81.9 tok/s (2K), 80.2 tok/s (8K), 75.2 tok/s (32K), 69.6 tok/s (64K)** — a **2.7–4.5× speedup** over serial encode — using **108.8 MiB** of transient chunk VRAM (fits ≤5,080 MiB by borrowing from the 1,200 MiB expert cache during prefill; 64K requires K4V2 KV).

### Key Bottleneck Findings from `nsys`
1. **Why `blk8` loses on MoE (`40.8–49.9` vs `12.4–13.5 ms/tok`)**: `ink_launch_exl3_moe_ntok` (`kernels.cu:5699`) sets `v2_ok = (v2_env > 0) && nky == 1 && v2_descs_ok`. Under `INKWELL_MOE_NKY=2`, `v2_ok` is false and it falls back to `inkwell_exl3_gemv_moe_ntok`, which lacks SASS `ch2nopless` scheduling and loops over consumers `cc < nc` with no register tiling.
2. **Where `blk8` ALREADY wins**:
   - **Dense EXL3**: drops from **13.25 → 9.15 ms/tok** (1.45×) at just `NT=8`, even while wastefully running `lm_head` on all 8 tokens (`11.16 ms/blk = 1.40 ms/tok` — 100% avoidable during prompt encode!).
   - **Controller/Mix/Router**: drops from **3.61 → 0.41 ms/tok** (8.8×) because `mmq` batches 8 tokens into GEMM.
   - **MoE ccopy**: drops from **8.7–11.2 → 4.0–5.1 ms/tok** (2.1×) because 8 tokens share union experts (~35 unique experts vs 64 selections).
3. **Hidden `blk8` attention tax at 8K**: `k_get_rows_float_vec` takes **4.60 ms/tok** at 8K because `step_block` materializes the full `[n_kv, nh]` relative-bias table via `get_rows` + `permute` + `cont` separately for each of the 8 tokens.

## 2. Exactness: `step_block` vs Sequential `step`

### 2a. Pre-existing `step_block` Bugs Found During Testing
Running `step_block` on real prompts uncovered 3 bugs on `master`:
1. **`crates/inkwell-cuda/src/kernels.cu:5741-5758`** (`ink_launch_exl3_moe_ntok`): `skipmode=2` (cache **misses**) was launched *before* `cudaStreamWaitEvent(st, ev_join)` and `skipmode=1` (hits) *after*, racing with the async miss copy on `ss`. Swapping `skipmode=1` before `ev_join` and `skipmode=2` after fixes it.
2. **`crates/inkwell-run/src/runner.rs:1747`** (`Runner::clip`): fused `clip_l2_cb` only handles `ne[2]==1`, corrupting `NT=8` when `scale.is_some()`.
3. **`crates/inkwell-run/src/runner.rs:1951,1990`** (`Runner::control`): `ctrl_apply_cb` and `view1` assumed `nt==1`, corrupting `NT=8` loop passes 1–2.

### 2c. Which Kernels Differ in FP Order?
Every kernel where `step_block` differs from `step`:
1. **Dense EXL3 (`proj8`, `lm_head`)**: `step` runs `ch2dense` (`inkwell_exl3_gemv_sf_ch2`, fixed split-K); `step_block` runs `inkwell_exl3_gemv_dp4a_ntok` with dynamic `nky ∈ {1,2,4,8}` and `had_out` reduction.
2. **MoE EXL3 (`gate`/`up`/`down`)**: `step` runs `ch2nopless` (`inkwell_exl3_gemv_moe_s`); `step_block` runs `inkwell_exl3_gemv_moe_ntok` with `nky=2` using **`atomicAdd`** into `dn_e` (non-deterministic across CTAs).
3. **`mix8` vs `mix_cb`**: `step` runs fused `inkwell_mix_fused`; `step_block` runs unfused ggml `rms_norm` + `mmq` (`sgemm`) + `soft_max` + `mmq`.
4. **Controller (`control`) & Clip (`clip`)**: `step` runs fused `rms2down` + `ctrl_apply_cb` and `clip_l2_cb`; `step_block` falls back to unfused ggml ops.
5. **Router (`ffn_gate_inp`)**: `step` runs fused `inkwell_gate_router_fused_exact`; `step_block` runs `mmq` (`ne1=8`) + `sigmoid` + `add` + `argsort_top_k`.
6. **Attention & Conv**: `step_block` calls the **exact same** `inkwell_conv_step` and `ink_launch_attn_decode` kernels per token — they do *not* introduce new FP-order noise, only propagate upstream diffs.

**Why `K_max_abs = 10.83` at 512 tokens**: a `~3e-4` FP-order diff occasionally flips a MoE top-8 router boundary on one of the `512 × 48 = 24,576` token-visits, causing an `O(1)` shift on that token's K/V while preserving distribution quality (`KLD = 3.53e-5`).

## 3. Design of a Real Chunked Prefill (`C = 64–512`)

### 3a. Loop-MoE Recurrence Analysis (What Is Actually Sequential?)
Tracing `Runner::step` (`runner.rs:2444-2481`) and `Runner::step_block` (`runner.rs:3525-3563`):
- **Loop-MoE controller / bank / msg / residual-block state is 100% PER-TOKEN, NOT sequential across tokens.**
  - `prelude`, `prelude_blocks`, `bank`, `bank_out`, `msgs = mix(bank, bank_out, msg_query)`, and `control(inp, msg, l, j)` have zero cross-token dependencies. All `C` tokens advance together through `prelude (L0..5)` → `pass 0 (L6..17)` → `pass 1 (L6..17)` → `pass 2 (L6..17)` → `tail (L18..23)`.
- **Short causal depthwise convolutions (`sconv_l_cache = 4`) are FIR filters, NOT sequential IIR recurrences.**
  - `inkwell_conv_step` (`kernels.cu:5363-5393`) computes `out[t] = (x[t] + sum_{lag=0..3} (w[lag] + dyn[t,lag]) * x[t-lag]) * scale`.
  - Given the 3-token prefix in `conv_state[slot]` and the chunk input `X[C, ch]`, all `C` output tokens can be computed **in parallel in one kernel**, and `conv_state[slot]` is simply overwritten with `X[C-3..C]`. Bitwise-identical to sequential `inkwell_conv_step`!
- **Causal attention + KV cache** is the only true cross-token dependency, and within a chunk of `C` tokens it is computed in parallel via causal tiled FlashAttention against `KV[0 .. pos+C)`.

2. **Flash Prefill Attention (`flash_prefill_attn`, `nh=24, hkv=8, hd=128`, F16 KV, `rel1024+sink`)**:
   - Prototype (1 head/CTA, un-tiled QKᵀ): **0.22–0.24 TFLOP/s** (`55.6 us/tok` SWA @ `C=512`; `1,680 us/tok` Full @ 32K).
   - **Key architectural upgrade**: GQA-3 sharing (1 CTA processes 3 Q-heads per KV-head → 3× fewer K/V loads) + 2D register-tiled QKᵀ and PV GEMM (`BQ=32, BK=32`, FP32 smem staging) + on-the-fly relative bias (`dot(r_q[16], rel_bias[dist, 16])` instead of materializing a `[C, 24, 1025]` table = 48 MiB saved!) brings sm_61 FlashAttention to **~1.5 TFLOP/s** (matching `exl3_gemm_smem`'s 1.39 TFLOP/s FP32 FMA rate on the same GPU).

### 3c. Critical Hardware Finding: PCIe H2D Streaming of the 14 Host-Pinned MoE Layers
At `C ≥ 64`, `8C` expert selections activate ~126–128 out of 128 experts per layer. Across the 18 host-pinned layer-visits (`L0..5` = 1,333 MiB; `L16..17 × 3 passes` = 2,166 MiB; `L18..23` = 1,415 MiB), total expert weights are **4,914 MiB/chunk** (0% cache) or **~3,440 MiB/chunk** (~30% hit rate with 900 MiB cache):
- At PCIe 3.0 x16 pinned H2D (~11.5 MiB/ms):
  - `C = 64`: **4.67–6.68 ms/tok** of PCIe H2D
  - `C = 128`: **2.34–3.34 ms/tok**
  - `C = 256`: **1.17–1.67 ms/tok**
  - `C = 512`: **0.58–0.83 ms/tok**
- **With double-buffered async H2D** (copying expert batch `b+1` into a 32 MiB staging ring on the copy stream while computing GEMM on expert batch `b`), **100% of PCIe H2D is overlapped behind MoE GEMM compute** at `C = 256–512`! Larger `C` is doubly essential: it amortizes both trellis decode (`M_exp = C/16`) and PCIe weight transfer.

### 3d. Component Architecture (Ranked by Impact)

1. **Grouped-by-Expert Batched EXL3 GEMM (Rank 1 — biggest win, ~65% of decode time)**:
   - Sort `8C` `(token, slot)` pairs by `expert_id ∈ [0..127]` on GPU (counting sort / CUB-style 128-bin radix in 1 kernel, `<0.02 ms`).
   - Process experts in **4 tiles of 32 experts** (`~2C` expanded tokens/tile) to cap MoE scratch at **31.5 MiB** at `C=512` (vs 126 MiB untiled).
   - Launch one grouped grid across all active experts in the tile (`grid = (N/64, total_M_tiles)`), eliminating the 1.8-wave tail effect of single-expert launches.
   - For the 14 host-pinned layers, double-buffer H2D of tile `t+1` (32 experts, ~8–10 MiB) on side stream `ss` into a 32 MiB staging ring while computing tile `t` on main stream `st` (or hit directly in the existing `kslot` cache when resident).
2. **Batched Dense EXL3 GEMM + Skip `lm_head` on Prompt Tokens (Rank 2 — 7× dense speedup)**:
   - Replace `exl3_gemv_ntok` (`NT=8` with per-weight scratch) with `exl3_gemm_smem<64, 64>` sharing **one** reusable prefill scratch buffer.
   - Compute `lm_head` **only on the final prompt token** (`pos = N-1`), saving `1.47 ms/tok` and `51.5 MiB` of `lm_head` scratch.
   - `2.548 GFLOP/tok` dense EXL3 across 48 visits drops from **13.25 → 1.85–1.90 ms/tok**.
3. **GQA-3 Tiled Flash Prefill Attention (Rank 3 — essential for ≥8K)**:
   - One CTA per `(q_tile=32, kv_head=0..7)` loads K/V once for all 3 GQA heads (`BQ=32, BK=32`).
   - Handles both SWA (`slot = k % 1024`, matching `t3/swa-ring-fix`) and Full attention (`slot = k`), causal mask `k ≤ q`, sink logit `0` (subtracted from bias), and on-the-fly relative bias for `dist < 1024` (0 MiB rel-table VRAM!).
4. **Parallel Causal FIR Conv + Batched Controller/Mix (Rank 4 — eliminates serial loops & graph rebuilds)**:
   - `inkwell_conv_prefill`: 1 kernel over `[C, ch]` reads `conv_state[slot]` for `t < 3` and `X[t-lag]` for `t ≥ lag`, writes `conv_state[slot] = X[C-3..C]`.
   - Direct CUDA-driver / Rust chunk loop (no 32K-node ggml graph rebuild per chunk!).

## 4. Exactness Policy Options

| Policy | What It Requires | Speed / Engineering Cost | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(i) Bitwise-identical prefill** | Every kernel must match `step()`'s exact FP32 reduction tree: (1) `exl3_gemm_smem` must match `ch2dense`/`ch2nopless` split-K order (actually **achievable** for EXL3 GEMM — `prefill_kbench` already hit `0 bit_diff` vs `exl3_ref_m1` by accumulating `k=0..tk-1` sequentially per thread!); (2) FlashAttention **cannot** be bitwise-identical to `attn_decode_splitw` (online softmax rescaling vs two-pass split-K reduction); (3) ggml `mmq` (`ne1=C`) uses cuBLAS SGEMM/MMQ instead of `mmvf` GEMV (`ne1=1`), which is never bitwise-identical. | Forcing bitwise identity on attention and small dense `mmq` would forbid FlashAttention and batched cuBLAS/MMQ, sacrificing **>50% of prefill speedup** at ≥8K and adding massive maintenance burden whenever decode kernels change. | **Not recommended** |
| **(ii) FP-order prefill gated by teacher-forced KLD** | Allow standard FP32 associativity differences in batched GEMM and FlashAttention, while keeping all math formulas exact (no reduced precision, no `atomicAdd` non-determinism in MoE). Gate every prefill PR with: (1) per-kernel unit test `max_abs < 1e-4` vs `step()` reference; (2) end-to-end teacher-forced KLD on first generated token **< 1e-3** (and mean KLD across 64 teacher-forced tokens **< 5e-4**) at ctx=512 and ctx=4K (post `t3/swa-ring-fix`); (3) killswitch `INKWELL_NO_PREFILL=1` restoring bit-exact serial `step()` (`md5=5cbe8e06`). | **Zero speed penalty**; full 80+ tok/s prefill. Matches the `3.5e-5`–`4.2e-4` KLD already observed on `step_block`. | **Recommended** |

## 5. Concrete Implementation Plan

One killswitch env var per milestone (`INKWELL_PREFILL=1` opt-in during development, then `INKWELL_NO_PREFILL=1` killswitch once gated). No megakernels; each op is a clean, standalone CUDA kernel.

### Phase 0: Immediate Fixes & Quick Win (0.5 day)
1. **Fix the 3 `step_block` bugs** (`crates/inkwell-cuda/src/kernels.cu:5741`, `crates/inkwell-run/src/runner.rs:1747,1951`) so `VTEST8` and spec-decode verify are sound.
2. **Use `step_id` (skip logit D2H + `lm_head` on prompt tokens `0..N-2`)** in `inkwell-cli/src/main.rs:299`: immediate free **+15% serial prefill speedup** (32.6 → ~27.8 ms/tok), 100% bitwise-identical (`md5=5cbe8e06`).

### Phase 1: Core Prefill Kernels (`crates/inkwell-cuda/src/prefill.cu` + FFI in `crates/inkwell-cuda/src/lib.rs`)
1. `inkwell_exl3_gemm_smem<BM, BN=64>`: register-tiled batched EXL3 GEMM (starting from `/tmp/prefill-study/prefill_kbench.cu`, adding `suh`/`svh` Hadamard in/out + `exl3v2` paired-window decode). Unit test: `0 bit_diff` vs `exl3_ref_m1`.
2. `inkwell_exl3_moe_group_sort` + `inkwell_exl3_moe_grouped_gemm`: 128-bin GPU counting sort of `8C` routed pairs, 4-tile (32-expert) batched execution, double-buffered async H2D staging ring (`32 MiB`) on side stream `ss` for host-pinned layers, fused SiTU + weighted scatter-add. No `atomicAdd` along K (deterministic).
3. `inkwell_conv_prefill`: parallel causal depthwise FIR conv (`sconv_l_cache=4`, static + dynamic per-head taps) over `[C, ch]` + `conv_state` update. Unit test: bitwise-identical to `C` sequential `inkwell_conv_step` calls.
4. `inkwell_flash_prefill_gqa3`: GQA-3 register-tiled FlashAttention (`BQ=32, BK=32`, F16 KV, SWA ring `k % 1024` + Full, causal mask, sink logit, on-the-fly `rel_bias` dot product). Unit test: `max_abs < 5e-5` vs `attn_decode_splitw`.

### Phase 2: Prefill Driver & Memory Management (`crates/inkwell-run/src/prefill.rs`, `runner.rs`, `main.rs`)
1. Add `PrefillArena` (`109 MiB` at `C=512`, `70 MiB` at `C=256`, configurable via `INKWELL_PREFILL_CHUNK`) carved from the `vramcap` expert-cache budget during prefill and released back to the decode expert cache before token 0.
2. Implement `Runner::prefill(&mut self, ids: &[u32]) -> Vec<f32>` driving the 48 visits directly via CUDA stream launches (no 32K-node ggml graph rebuilds):
   - Prelude `L0..5` → Loop `pass 0..3` (`L6..17`, maintaining per-chunk `blocks`/`bank`/`bank_out` in `PrefillArena`) → Tail `L18..23` → final token `mix` + `output_norm` + `lm_head`.
   - Falls back to serial `step()` when `ids.len() < 16` or `INKWELL_NO_PREFILL=1`.
3. Wire into `inkwell-cli/src/main.rs` (`encode` loop) and add a `prefill_kld` gate test verifying:
   - Peak VRAM ≤ 5,080 MiB at ctx=512, 2K, 8K, 32K (and 64K with K4V2).
   - Teacher-forced KLD < `1e-3` (first token) and < `5e-4` (mean over 64 tokens) vs serial `step()` at ctx=512 and ctx=4K (after `t3/swa-ring-fix`).
   - Serial decode speed & `md5=5cbe8e06` completely unchanged when `INKWELL_NO_PREFILL=1`.
